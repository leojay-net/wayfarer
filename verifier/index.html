Wayfarer receipt verifier

Prototype. Testnet receipts only. Unaudited. Everything runs in this page: the ML-DSA-65 signatures are checked by JavaScript in your browser, nothing is uploaded. The "against the chain" button asks the public NEAR testnet RPC for the transaction and the owner's keys. A receipt proves the request, the mandate and the on-chain events agree; it does not prove who pressed the button or that goods were delivered.
